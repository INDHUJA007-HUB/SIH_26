Concept Note 
Proposal for Pilot Implementation of Experiential Learning Programmes 
(ELPs) [ Industry – Academia Collaboration] 
 1. Background 
In accordance with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, AICTE proposes 
to introduce Experiential Learning Programmes (ELPs) as a pilot initiative 
to bridge the gap between academic learning and workplace application. The 
goal is to make students industry -ready by enabling them to learn directly 
within industrial environments , gain hands-on experience , and build 
employable skills while studying. 
2. Objective 
To create a structured and mutually beneficial partnership between higher 
education institutions and industries, ensuring that students gain real -time 
industrial exposure and that industries participate in d eveloping the next 
generation of skilled professionals. 
3. Implementation Framework 
As a pilot project, select AICTE-approved institutions situated in regions with 
a strong industrial base  will be allowed to offer these programmes in 
Institutions only thro ugh a formal MoU  with identified  industries 
collaboration. 
 
The MoU shall clearly specify: 
 The curriculum framework , jointly designed and approved by both 
parties. 
 Roles and responsibilities of the institution and the industry. 
 Assessment and certification mechanisms. 
 Placement commitments after programme completion. 
4. Eligibility 
Institutions: 
 AICTE-approved institutions should have listed in NIRF 
(Engineering/Management) or with proven track records of industry 
collaboration. 
Industry Partners: 
 Large In dustry / Corporation having turnover of about ₹100 crore  
(average over 3 years).   
=== PDF PAGE 20 ===
19 
 
 Dedicated training infrastructure, laboratories, and mentors to host 
students. 
 Every attempt to provide the placement or absorption opportunities 
upon successful completion. 
5. Programme Design and Delivery 
 Curriculum Development: Jointly framed by academic and industry 
experts, ensuring relevance to current and emerging technologies. 
 Structure: 
o Upto 50% experiential/work-based learning at industry sites. 
o Remaining academic components at the institution, blending 
theory and application. 
o Integration of project -based learning, internships, and live 
industry assignments. 
 Faculty Composition: 
o Academic instructors from the institution. 
o Industry mentors  to co -teach and ev aluate practical 
components. 
 Student Eligibility: 
o Students admitted through a transparent, merit-based system. 
o Provision for working professionals or upskilling candidates 
where applicable. 
6. Infrastructure Norms 
Institutions and industries must jointly ensure: 
 Smart Classrooms and ICT-based learning systems. 
 Laboratories (physical or remote) for hands-on experimentation. 
 Immersive learning setups  such as AR/VR/XR -enabled simulation 
labs. 
 Workplace immersion centres within industry premises. 
 Digital platfo rms for continuous monitoring, attendance, and 
assessment. 
 Adequate student support systems , hostels or transport, and safety 
provisions during industrial visits/training. 
7. Assessment and Certification 
 Continuous assessment  through projects, industry tasks, and 
performance logs. 
 Joint evaluation by institutional faculty and industry mentors. 
 Summative assessments  conducted as per AICTE -approved 
examination norms. 
 Students shall receive a degree/diploma/certificate as per AIC TE 
nomenclature, clearly indicating the experiential component.

8. Quality Assurance and Monitoring 
 A Joint Academic Committee (JAC) comprising representatives from 
both the institution and the industry shall oversee curriculum delivery, 
attendance, safety, and quality. 
 Periodic reporting to AICTE with data on student performance, 
innovation outcomes, and placement records. 
 Annual review by AICTE; successful pilots to be recommended for 
national rollout. 
9. Duration and Pilot Period 
 Programme duration: As p er AICTE’s approved structure for the 
respective level (Diploma/UG/PG) and in line with NCrF. 
10. Expected Outcomes 
 Industry-ready graduates with practical and analytical competencies. 
 Strengthened industry–academia partnerships leading to co-creation 
of innovation ecosystems. 
 Creation of a national model for work -integrated technical 
education aligned with NEP 2020 and Skill India objectives.
