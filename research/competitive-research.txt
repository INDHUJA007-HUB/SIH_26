1. Executive summary
1.1 Verdict in six lines
1. Concept: not unique. PS 26261 itself deﬁnes the six statuses, the safeguards and the evaluation metrics; at least ﬁve public implementations already exist for
this PS.
2. Closest competitor:Pramana (472 commits, 162 tests, real CCA-root signature checks, C2PA, revision "time machine", ﬁne-tuned local extractor,
encryption/retention). Do not try to out-forensic it in the time you have.
3. Your real edge: governance of the regulation itself (versioned rules, addenda, impact analysis), honest evaluation, and remediation (what would make thispass), not just detection.
4. Biggest new ﬁnding: the APH 2024-27 is a three-year handbook that is changed by annual addenda — e.g. the 2025-26 addendum reportedly removed"adjunct faculty" and set Professor-of-Practice to 20% (5% reserved for women) [R], and the 2026-27 addendum reportedly raised supernumerary seats to 25%
and requires "nil deﬁciency based on self-disclosure" [R]. Any engine with a single frozen rule ﬁle is wrong by design. Yours can be right by design.

5. Your spec has accuracy and security problems (cadre formula, adjunct caps now outdated, page-number conﬂicts, /365.25 date bug, role chosen by theclient at login, raw PAN, AGPL dependencies, no encryption/retention). All are ﬁxable and are listed in Section 3.
6. Benchmark numbers in v2.0.0 are targets, not results. Present them as targets until measured, with conﬁdence intervals and a false-accusation rate.

2.2 Context that shapes the right design
AICTE already runs e-governance for approvals since January 2010 [V] (S18). The portal produces deﬁciency reports based on self-disclosure[V] (S19). The
gap is authenticity and cross-checking, not data entry.
The handbook is multi-year but not static. APH 2024-27 is a three-year handbook [V] (S17); AICTE's 2025-26 page links an "Addendum/Corrigendum 2025-26
of APH 2024-27" [V] (S25); a 2026-27 addendum also exists [R] (S14). Rules therefore need effective-cycle layering.
2025-26 addendum (reported): "Adjunct Faculty" (part-time, up to 15% of required faculty) done away with; "Professor of Practice" up to 20% of requiredfaculty (cadre) — 5% exclusively reserved for women PoPs on regular basis [R] (S13). The original APH 2024-27 text allowed adjunct or PoP up to 15% with
special wording for Design and Planning [V] (S15, partial text).
2026-27 addendum (reported): supernumerary seats up to 25% for management/design and other technical programmes; institutions required to show "nil
deﬁciency based on self disclosure" and submit student details [R] (S14).

=== PDF PAGE 4 ===
Mandatory website disclosure (Annexure-18): AICTE's own 2026-27 EoA letter says institutions must disclose, at a prominent place on their website, (i)department-wise infrastructure with approved courses/intake, (ii) faculty details with qualiﬁcation, tenure, experience, Student:Faculty Ratio and Cadre Ratio,
(iii) audited ﬁnancial statements for the last 3 years [V] (S12). The same letter cites Annexure-18 at page 180[V] — this conﬂicts with page numbers in yourv2.0.0 (see C-06).
Ghost faculty is a recognised problem. The Education Ministry has approved voluntary Aadhaar-based authentication for AICTE-regulated institutions,explicitly to curb ghost faculty [V] (S20). Voluntary ⇒  you cannot assume it; your PAN/HMAC fallback is justiﬁed.
Real consent-based issuer access exists — DigiLocker Requester APIs (list issued documents, fetch ﬁle/XML with integrity HMAC) [V] (S21). This is the
legitimate route to "verify at the issuer" without scraping.
Demand signal: other institutions are starting to build AI document veriﬁcation after ﬁnding manipulated documents (e.g., Delhi University announced plans
in July 2026) [V] (S22).
Idea-stage competition on this PS is thin (25 ideas as of 23 Oct per a mirror) [R] (S1/S2), but several complete public implementations exist, so ﬁnale-stagecompetition is not thin.
An earlier AICTE approval-portal PS on sih.gov.in asked for evaluator matching, real-time tracking and "digital dimension tracking of infrastructure" [V] (S24).The same themes (workﬂow, dimension checking) keep recurring, which supports adding ﬂoor-plan/dimension evidence (U-09).

6. Corrected evaluation protocol — how to show measured results honestly
6.1 Principles
1. Targets are not results. Show Target | Measured | 95% CI | n side by side.
2. "Held-out" must mean something. Different generator seeds and layout families the extractor never saw during development.
3. Hard negatives are ﬁrst-class. A system that never accuses a genuine applicant is the credible one.
4. Intervals everywhere. With 15 positives and 35 negatives, point estimates alone mislead.
5. No tuning on the test set. Freeze code/thresholds at a tagged commit before the test run.
6. Publish the generator and seeds so anyone can rerun (make eval SEED=…).
7. Say plainly that data is synthetic. The brief requires authorized/anonymized/synthetic data [V] (S1); claim generalisation only as far as you tested it.

6.6 Time-and-motion protocol for metric 7b
1. Recruit volunteers; disclose that they are not AICTE oﬃcers unless you can recruit real ones (external-validity limit).
2. Same 10 packets per participant; counter-balanced order (manual-ﬁrst vs assisted-ﬁrst).

4. Record time and correctness (a fast wrong decision is not a saving).
5. Report median and IQR of time per packet; paired Wilcoxon signed-rank; present as "estimated reduction in this study" with n.
6. Keep the old "18 min vs 2.5 min" as an assumption label only if you cannot run the study.

7.5 Likely jury questions and answers
# Question Answer (short)
1 "Similar tools already exist."
Yes — several public prototypes target this PS. Ours differs in governing the rulebook (layered addenda, impact simulation,
maker-checker), quantiﬁed remediation, honest veriﬁcation tiers and a measured false-accusation rate. We adopted the parity
items that matter (DSC veriﬁcation, revision diff, encryption).
2"Where is the AI?" OCR/classiﬁcation, near-duplicate image matching, optional grounded local LLM extraction, calibrated anomaly scoring. Final
compliance decisions are deterministic and explainable by design (R19/R20).
3"Does it call real government
registries?"
Not in the prototype — adapters are simulated and watermarked. The matrix shows the production path per document (oﬄine-
veriﬁable signals, DigiLocker consent-pull, public lookups). We do not assume restricted databases (brief R17).
4"What when the handbook
changes?"
Publish an overlay; the impact simulator shows who ﬂips; a second approver signs it; old decisions remain reproducible with the
old stack.
5 "How do you avoid accusing
genuine applicants?"
Corroboration rule, calibrated severity, benign-explanation display, abstention to manual review, and we publish FAR with
intervals.
6"Is data safe?" Server-side roles, envelope encryption, sandboxed parsing, retention with audited purge, no raw government IDs, hash-chained
audit with external anchoring.
7 "How does it integrate withthe AICTE portal?" Stateless evaluator + REST API + adapter contract; SSO placeholder; reads institutional records through authorised interfaces;PostgreSQL path.
8 "Your data is synthetic." By the brief's own rules; we use template-shift splits, hard negatives, a red-team matrix with blind spots, plus real DSC-signed
government PDFs for signature checks. Intervals reﬂect sample size.
